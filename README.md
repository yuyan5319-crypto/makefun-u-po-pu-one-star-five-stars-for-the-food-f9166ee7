A single-sentence review of planet Earth, written in the voice of an alien
tourist: one star overall, five stars for the food.

Run `python3 main.py` and it writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`
(`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` overrides the output directory for local testing).
The program validates the review before writing: one sentence, one line,
1–200 characters, no padding or double spaces.

## The review

> One star: your gravity bruised all four of my knees, your weather changes
> its mind hourly, and yet the noodles were so good I called off my invasion
> to finish the bowl.

## Where the joke comes from

The review works because the alien is a tourist first and an invader second:
every complaint is a body-count of ordinary Earth annoyances — gravity that
bruises four knees, weather that changes its mind hourly — and then the
invasion itself gets cancelled for the sake of one bowl of noodles. The
five-star food rating is not a consolation prize; it is the reason the
planet survives. That is the whole reversal in one sentence: the harshest
critic on the page is also Earth's most easily bribed defender — feed him
once, and the fleet turns around.
